Key Takeaways

Patterns worth acting on when planning the next campaign wave.

65+ & 18–25
The two age extremes convert best (46.8% and 20.9%), while the 36–55 working-age bracket lags at 8–9%.
Students & Retirees
These two job categories lead conversion at 31.4% and 25.2% — both well above the 11.3% overall average.
Cellular > Telephone
Mobile contact converts at 14.7% vs. 5.2% for landline — channel choice alone nearly triples response.
Fewer Touches Win
Rate falls from 13.0% on the first contact to under 5% by the 8th — over-contacting erodes response.
Prior Success = 65%
A previously successful campaign outcome predicts a 65.1% re-subscription rate, by far the strongest signal available.
Education Trend
Subscription rate rises with education, from 7.8% (basic 9y) to 13.7% (university degree).
